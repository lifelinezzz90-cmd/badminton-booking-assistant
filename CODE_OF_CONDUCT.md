# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, give constructive feedback, assume good intent, protect other people's privacy, and focus discussion on improving the project. Do not publish credentials, personal data, private logs, targeted harassment, threats, or sexualized content.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, commits, code, issues, or other contributions that violate this code and may temporarily or permanently ban abusive participants. Report conduct concerns privately through the contact method in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers will protect reporter privacy where reasonably possible.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1.
